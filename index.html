§ 01 The Route

Start: Tong, 73rd St & 37th Ave (3:30 PM). End: Birria-Landia, Roosevelt & 78th St. Everything is walkable in one continuous loop. Two seated stops in the middle — Playground and Khampa Kitchen — prevent the crawl from becoming a forced march.

§ 02 How to Run This With Six People

  • Order 2–3 dishes per stop, max. The goal is variety, not volume.
  • Share everything. Family-style at every stop, no exceptions.
  • One person orders, one person pays — rotate. Avoids 6-way Venmo chaos at every stop.
  • 20–30 minutes per stop. If you're sitting longer than that, you're over-ordering.
  • Khao Kang closes at 7 PM. Arrive there no later than 6:30 PM — that's the timing constraint that drives the whole crawl.
  • Bring cash. Tong, Birria-Landia, and some smaller spots are cash-friendly or cash-only.

§ 06 Crawl Strategy

Biggest mistake to avoid

Ordering full meals. The number-one way to ruin a 6-stop crawl is treating Stop 2 like dinner. Two or three plates per stop, share everything, keep moving.

Ordering rhythm for six people

  • At every stop, designate one orderer and one payer. Rotate stops.
  • Order the recommended dishes plus one wildcard the group hasn't tried.
  • If a stop's plates are small (Tong, Khampa), double the orders. If they're large (Khao Kang), don't.

Worth spontaneously adding

  • Sugarcane juice vendors (cash, $4–5)
  • Grilled corn or skewer carts on 74th St
  • Bangladeshi tea stalls along 73rd St for chai
  • A scoop of kulfi or falooda if anyone's still standing

Why this lineup works

Six cuisines, no repetition. Each stop hits a different flavor register: snacky → grilled → herbal/acidic → dumpling → curry/braise → braised-beef-and-cheese. The route is a tight loop — no doubling back, no transit.
